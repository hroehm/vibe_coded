# 🕹️ The Arcade Architect: Professional Development

**Welcome to the next level of Professional Development.**

For decades, the educational realm has been trapped in the **"Static Wastes"**—an endless, gray desert of flat PDFs, multiple-choice worksheets, and passive "sit-and-get" lectures[cite: 2]. 

You are a **Vibe-Coder**, a newly awakened digital architect who can shape interactive reality using only natural language[cite: 2]. Your overarching mission is to restore kinetic energy to the realm by forging dynamic, standalone artifacts and broadcasting them across the educational grid[cite: 2].

In this 9-quest journey, you will learn to use AI (like Google Canvas) to instantly transform your existing curriculum into interactive, retro-arcade review games. **Zero coding experience is required.** If you can type a prompt, you can build a game.

---

## 🐾 Meet Your Guide
Throughout your journey, you will be accompanied by **Miss Mabel**, a pixelated, slightly timid Basset Hound who lives in the corner of your interface[cite: 2]. She serves as your tutorial guide and error-handler. Miss Mabel hides under a digital desk with a quiet whimper if a layout breaks, but howls a cheerful, synthesized 8-bit tune when your code successfully renders![cite: 2]

---

## 🗺️ Select Your Level

The Arcade Architect is divided into three distinct worlds. You must clear them in order. 

*   **[World 1: The Local Sandbox](world-1.md)** 
    *   *Goal:* Prove vibe-coding is safe and fast[cite: 2]. Go from a prompt to a functioning local `.html` file you can run on your own machine[cite: 2].
*   **[World 2: The Digital Broadcast](world-2.md)** 
    *   *Goal:* Move the activity out of the local environment and make it accessible to students on the web[cite: 2].
*   **[World 3: The Arcade Architect](world-3.md)**
    *   *Goal:* Build a fully playable, offline-capable review game, refine it for local system storage, and deploy it to the web[cite: 2].
